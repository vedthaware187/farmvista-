Crop Price Forecasting & Advanced Crop Sowing Analysis
A web-based platform leveraging Machine Learning to forecast the prices of 22+ essential agri-horticultural commodities, while also providing an advanced sowing analysis and guidance system to enhance agricultural efficiency.

Overview
This project aims to:

Forecast commodity prices using data from the Department of Consumer Affairs, historical price trends, seasonality, market intelligence, and crop production estimates.
Provide Advanced Crop Sowing Analysis, guiding farmers on the best crops to plant in various districts and offering insights on market demand.
Support market interventions by indicating when buffer stocks should be released to stabilize market prices and reduce volatility.
Ultimately, the platform empowers stakeholders (farmers, policymakers, etc.) to make data-driven decisions that foster market stability and agricultural productivity.

Key Features
ML-Based Price Forecasting

Predicts commodity prices (e.g., onion, potato, pulses, etc.) over user-specified time periods.
Uses seasonality, historical data, and market trends to increase accuracy.
Commodity Insights

Showcases 25+ initial commodities with detailed information (optimal growing conditions, temperature ranges, market demand, etc.).
Advanced Sowing Guidance

Provides district-wise crop recommendations.
Analyzes climate, soil, and regional factors to suggest the best sowing strategy.
Buffer Stock Intervention

Alerts users when to release buffer stocks to stabilize prices and reduce volatility.
Interactive User Interface

Simple dropdown to select commodity and number of days for price forecasting.
Card-based layout for commodity details and sowing guidelines.
Map-based district selection for region-specific recommendations.

Usage
Price Forecasting

In the “Crop Price Forecasting” interface, select a commodity (e.g., Rice), enter the number of days, and click Submit to view price predictions.
Explore Commodities

Visit the “Initial 25 Commodities” section to learn about each crop’s optimal growing conditions, seasonality, and demand.
Advanced Sowing Analysis

Click on a Crop or District in the “Advanced Crop Sowing Analysis” dashboard to explore recommendations, best practices, and region-specific data.
Market Intervention Insights

Review forecasts and recommendations to decide on buffer stock releases for market stabilization.
